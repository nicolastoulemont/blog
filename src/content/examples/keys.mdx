---
title: 'Keys'
description: 'Keyboard keys and shortcuts in prose.'
---

A `<kbd>` element marks a key the reader presses. Join the keys of a shortcut with `+` outside the elements.

## Single key

Press <kbd>/</kbd> on the home page to search posts, and <kbd>Esc</kbd> to clear the search.

## Shortcut

Press <kbd>⌘</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> on macOS, or <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> + <kbd>K</kbd> elsewhere, to open the post palette.

## In a list

- <kbd>↑</kbd> and <kbd>↓</kbd> move the selection.
- <kbd>Enter</kbd> opens the selected post.
- <kbd>Shift</kbd> + <kbd>Tab</kbd> moves focus back.
