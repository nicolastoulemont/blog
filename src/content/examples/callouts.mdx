import { Callout } from '~/components'

A callout sets a passage apart from the prose. The variant picks the color and the label, and `label` overrides the label, such as in a French post.

## Note

<Callout>
  A note adds context the reader can skip. It is the default variant, so `<Callout>` needs
  no props.
</Callout>

## Definition

<Callout variant="definition">
  A definition states what a term means before the post uses it.
</Callout>

## Tip

<Callout variant="tip">
  A tip suggests a better way to do what the post describes.
</Callout>

## Warning

<Callout variant="warning">
  A warning flags a trap the reader can fall into, such as a breaking change or data loss.
</Callout>

## Custom label

<Callout variant="definition" label="Définition">
  `label` replaces the variant's label and keeps its color.
</Callout>
